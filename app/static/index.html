IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

Methods & limitations

What the prototype does

Digital PDFs use embedded text and numeric font metadata. Scanned pages use English Tesseract OCR. A small TF-IDF logistic classifier and document headings identify document types. Structured checks compare SKU quantities, unit prices, arithmetic and vendor accounts. Each finding retains page coordinates.

How to interpret findings

PDF extraction confidence is unavailable. OCR confidence reflects recognition, not authenticity. Classifier scores are uncalibrated. Numeric typography outliers are visual observations: formatting, scanning or legitimate emphasis can explain them.

Supported scope

English labelled vendor documents, one currency per linked comparison and SKU table rows ending in quantity, unit price and amount. Documents link through vendor and purchase-order identifiers. Only uploaded records inform comparisons.

Limitations

General layouts, handwriting, signature authentication, tax-law validation and bank ownership verification are unsupported. Partial receipts, document revisions and old vendor records require clarification. No finding establishes fraud. This local prototype has no multi-user authentication and should bind to localhost.

Data handling

Cases persist locally until deleted. Delete a case to remove its originals, page images and JSON. Hosted AI narration sends finding explanations only when explicitly requested and configured. All supplied demonstration documents are fictional.
